WSU Computer Labs — Student Awareness Campaign

Short-form content designed to make campus computer labs feel accessible, useful, and relevant to everyday student life.

Many students weren’t aware of the full range of lab resources available to them. I created approachable, student-focused content that highlighted where the labs are, what they offer, and why they matter during the semester.

Project Overview

Brand: Weber State University — Computer Labs

Content Type: Short-form social video & student-focused promos

Target Audience: Current WSU students & new/incoming students

Tools & Equipment: iPhone 15 Pro, gimbal, natural light, CapCut / Adobe Premiere

Goals

  • Increase awareness of campus lab locations and available resources
  • Make the labs feel less intimidating and more “for students”
  • Encourage students to see the labs as a study, printing, and project hub

Approach & Strategy

I focused on simple, clear, and visually guided storytelling: walking tours, “day in the life” style clips,
