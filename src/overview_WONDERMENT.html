Wonderment

UX / UI focused case study. Low–, medium–, and high-fidelity prototypes; UX research; built and iterated in Figma.

View Medium-Fidelity XD Prototype View High-Fidelity XD Prototype

Overview

Wonderment (by Kidnected World) is a platform that gives kids safe, guided spaces to take action in their communities, share work creatively, and collaborate globally. This case study summarizes the UX research and the progression from low-fi wireframes to a polished, responsive UI system.

Focus
UX Research · IA · UI System · Prototyping (Lo/Med/Hi)
Tools
Figma, FigJam, Google Workspace, basic analytics
Deliverables
Personas, task flows, clickable prototypes, usability study reports

Mission

The Wonderment by Kidnected World provides spaces for kids to take action in their local and global communities. Kids, schools and organizations come together to create a world of good. It’s a platform where kids can meet the world and others, share and collaborate creatively on a global level, and make tangible impact—all in a safe space designed for them.

Vision

Connect globally through tools, spaces, and experiences that deepen kids’ connection to the world as they expand their awareness of each other. Together we build an ecosystem where cultures are preserved and shared, technology strengthens rather than dilutes communities, everyone is a creator, and the things we create bring us together to make the world better in tangible ways.

Round 1

Testing Round 1 — Functionality & Display

Issues
  • Settings button didn’t do anything.
  • Some navbar items didn’t work properly.
  • Hamburger menu didn’t do anything.
Solutions
  • Removed settings & hamburger buttons.
  • Rewired button targets before next round.
Navbar Display Problems
  • Nav at bottom was often missed.
  • Upper navbar/logo cut off on phones.
Fixes
  • Lower navbar fixed on scroll.
  • Increased upper navbar height by 40px.
Content Issues
  • Confusion between Paths, Projects, Communities.
Fix
  • Added more explanatory content on homepage.

Round 2

Testing Round 2 — Bugs & Other Issues

Bugs
  • Prototype didn’t start on login screen.
  • Navbar/graph didn’t display on Project detail.
Fixes
  • Changed flow to start correctly.
  • Brought navbar above content layers.
Other Issues
  • ‘Respond to Path’ button hard to find.
  • Search bars lacked submit behavior.
Fixes
  • Increased button text size, adjusted colors.
  • Added keyboard handling to search bars.

Summary

Research Summary

  • App felt simple, straightforward, intuitive.
  • Minor early issues were fixed and didn’t reappear.
  • Later iterations focused on UX polish over functionality.
