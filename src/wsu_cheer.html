WSU Cheerleading — Nationals Hype Content

A set of short-form hype videos celebrating team culture, athleticism, and the lead-up to UCA Nationals.

I aimed to capture the energy and intensity of collegiate cheer, generating excitement among fans, alumni, and incoming athletes.

Project Overview

Brand: Weber State University Spirit Squad

Content Type: Hype reels, movement-driven edits

Audience: Students, athletes, cheer community

Tools Used: iPhone 15 Pro, Adobe Premiere, CapCut

Objectives

  • Elevate hype for Nationals season
  • Showcase athletic difficulty + team culture
  • Create shareable content for athletes and fans

Creative Strategy

I captured high-intensity training moments, tumbling sequences, partner stunt highlights, and behind-the-scenes clips. I paired these with dramatic audio and snappy transitions to heighten emotion and anticipation.
