Hey Dude Lifestyle Ad

A personality-driven video showcasing comfort, movement, and fun for the Hey Dude brand.

My goal was to create upbeat, personality-forward content that would feel authentic while aligning with their brand tone: casual, playful, easygoing.

Project Overview

Brand: Hey Dude

Content Type: Lifestyle short-form video

Audience: Gen-Z & young adults

Tools Used: iPhone 15 Pro, CapCut

Strategy

I built the video around movement and quick cuts to show the comfort and everyday wearability of the shoes. The tone is playful, authentic, and visually energetic.

Execution

  • Shot multiple angles to show movement
  • Used trending audio to boost discoverability
  • Added captions and jump cuts for pacing

A selection of content created for this campaign. Each piece was filmed, edited, and directed to match the brand tone and platform style.

Results

Strong engagement and positive brand response. Content performed above average for similar lifestyle assets.
