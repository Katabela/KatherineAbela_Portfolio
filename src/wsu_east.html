WSU EAST — Engineering, Applied Sciences & Technology Content

Relatable, trend-aware content to connect with engineering & technology students and showcase life inside EAST.

The brief was to move away from “stiff” academic messaging and towards content that feels like it’s made by and for students — while still highlighting labs, projects, and the unique culture within EAST.

Project Overview

Brand: Weber State University — EAST

Content Type: Student-facing, trend-aligned short-form videos

Audience: Current & prospective EAST students

Tools & Equipment: iPhone 15 Pro, tripod / handheld, CapCut, Adobe Premiere

Goals

  • Make EAST feel more approachable and student-centered
  • Show the real culture: labs, projects, collaboration, and campus life
  • Leverage trends without losing the integrity of the programs

Creative Strategy

I used trending audio, quick cuts, and light humor to showcase EAST life — from lab work and projects to hallway moments and teamwork. Each piece was built to be easily shareable and to make students think, “That’s literally us.”

  • Relatable, student POV filming style
  • Quick intros that hook within the first second
  • Text overlays to clarify programs, spaces, and opportunities
