Amazon Music Streaming Offer Promotion

A short-form promotional reel designed to boost awareness for Amazon Music’s 3-month free streaming trial.

I created a high-energy, scroll-stopping video that used trending audio, personality, and clean transitions to encourage sign-ups.

Project Overview

Brand: Amazon Music

Content Type: Short-form promotional video

Audience: Music lovers, casual listeners, new app users

Tools Used: iPhone 15 Pro, CapCut, Adobe Premiere

Goals

  • Promote the free 3-month trial
  • Create a visual hook within the first 1–2 seconds
  • Match the fun, energetic personality of Amazon Music

My Approach

I built the concept around authenticity — quick cuts, bold expressions, and a clean story beat showcasing why the offer is worth trying. I integrated trending audio, movement-based transitions, and on-screen text to increase retention.

Project Videos

A selection of content created for this campaign. Each piece was filmed, edited, and directed to match the brand tone and platform style.

Main reel created for this campaign.

Results

The content received strong engagement and positive comments from viewers, with high click-through interest in the offer. The brand noted that the promo performed well within their creator set for the campaign.
